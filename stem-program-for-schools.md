## Why STEM Education Matters More Than Ever

Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) are no longer subjects taught in isolation. Today, they are integrated into hands-on learning experiences where students design, build, experiment, and solve practical problems. Rather than memorising concepts, students actively explore robotics, coding, artificial intelligence, IoT, engineering design, drone technology, and automation through project-based activities.

This approach develops analytical thinking, collaboration, communication, creativity, and innovation while making learning more engaging and meaningful. Schools that embrace STEM programmes create learning environments where students become confident problem-solvers who are prepared for higher education and the rapidly evolving digital economy.
