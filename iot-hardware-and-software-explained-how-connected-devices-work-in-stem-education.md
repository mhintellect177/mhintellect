# IoT Hardware and Software Explained: How Connected Devices Work in STEM Education

From smart home appliances and fitness trackers to agricultural monitoring systems, connected devices are becoming an important part of everyday life. These technologies collect information, process data, and communicate with other systems to perform useful tasks. Understanding [**IoT hardware and software**](https://mhintellect.com/blogs/iot-hardware-software-explained) helps students explore how connected technology works and how it is applied in real-world environments.

For schools developing STEM programmes, learning about the Internet of Things (IoT) creates opportunities to connect electronics, programming, sensors, and data processing through practical classroom activities.

## Understanding How IoT Devices Work

To understand [**how do IoT devices work**](https://mhintellect.com/blogs/iot-hardware-software-explained), it is important to explore how different components communicate and operate together. An IoT device is a physical object equipped with electronics, software, and network connectivity that allows it to collect and exchange information.

Most connected devices perform four essential functions: sensing information from the physical environment, processing collected data, communicating with other devices or platforms, and taking action based on the information received.

For example, a smart irrigation system measures soil moisture, processes the readings, and activates a water valve when the soil becomes too dry. This demonstrates how connected technology can automate everyday activities.

## Key IoT Device Components

Understanding [**IoT device components**](https://mhintellect.com/blogs/iot-hardware-software-explained) helps students recognise the role of hardware in building connected systems. Each component contributes to the device's ability to collect, process and exchange information.

Some important components include:

- **Sensors:** Detect temperature, humidity, light, motion, and pressure from the surrounding environment.
- **Microcontrollers:** Process sensor readings and control the device's operations.
- **Connectivity modules:** Transfer information through Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, LoRaWAN, or cellular networks.
- **Actuators:** Perform physical actions such as operating motors, switching lights or opening water valves.
- **Power systems:** Provide and regulate the energy required for device operation.

Together, these components allow IoT devices to interact with their surroundings and perform automated functions.

## Understanding IoT Architecture

[**IoT architecture**](https://mhintellect.com/blogs/iot-hardware-software-explained) explains how different parts of a connected system work together. A common four-layer model includes the perception layer, network layer, processing layer, and application layer.

The perception layer collects information through sensors. The network layer transfers data between connected devices and platforms. The processing layer stores and analyses information, while the application layer presents results through dashboards, alerts, and applications.

This structure is used across smart agriculture, healthcare, industrial automation and smart city applications.
